## Optional product capabilities

- [ ] Privacy-conscious traffic analytics
- [ ] Spam-protected contact form
- [ ] Newsletter or update subscription
- [ ] Searchable certificates and courses
- [ ] Project filters by discipline and technology
- [ ] Theme preference

## Explicitly out of scope by default

- Internal employer screenshots or production data
- Private HR, payroll, or employee information
- Credential IDs that are not intended for public verification
- Visitor accounts
- Unmoderated comments
- Tracking that requires unnecessary personal data
